Equipment & facilities

A multi-purpose imaging-metrology laboratory supporting research and teaching across diverse sensors.

Core facility

Geo-Spatial Vision Metrology (GeoVM) Laboratory

The walls and ceiling contain some 500 signalized targets of different sizes used for instrument calibration, orientation, and performance evaluation. Several sizes of targets have been placed throughout the room to allow experiments to be performed with a wide range of imaging sensors such as passive cameras, time-of-flight range cameras, gaming sensors, laser scanners, and thermal cameras, and sensors with different fields of view, frame imaging cameras, and panoramic sensors. The constant temperature control in the room and the ability to control ambient light with light switches and blinds allow us to conduct high-precision metrology experiments.
