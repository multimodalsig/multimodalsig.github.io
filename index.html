Multimodal Spatial Imaging Group

Department of Geomatics Engineering · Schulich School of Engineering · University of Calgary

The Multimodal Spatial Imaging Group (MSIG) develops precision measurement methods and rigorous mathematical models for 3D reality capture using diverse imaging sensors: terrestrial laser scanners, digital cameras, range cameras, LiDAR systems, and fluoroscopic imagers.

Led by Dr. Derek Lichti, our program sits at the intersection of imaging metrology, sensor calibration, and 3D spatial data analysis. We build the geometric and stochastic models that let sensors deliver trustworthy measurements.
